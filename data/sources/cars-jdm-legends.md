# Car Legends — Spec Comparison (Dataset 2)

> Verified via web search against published/enthusiast-site and manufacturer specs (August 2026). Where sources disagreed, the most commonly cited figure was used and noted. Treat 0–60 and top-speed figures as representative for the trim listed, not a single official factory number — these vary by market (JDM/US/EU), test methodology, and model year even for the same nameplate. Where a real source simply didn't publish a figure (some 0–60 times, some curb weights on very old or very limited-run cars), that field is marked "not published" rather than guessed — a numeric dataset is only useful for charting if every number in it is real.

## Toyota Supra MK4 (A80) Turbo
- Category: JDM Legends
- Years: 1993–2002
- Engine: 2JZ-GTE, 3.0L twin-turbo inline-6
- Horsepower: 321 hp
- Torque: 315 lb-ft
- 0–60 mph: 4.6 s
- Top speed: 155 mph (electronically limited; unlimited US models observed near 177 mph)
- Curb weight: 3,560 lb
- Note: JDM output was understated under Japan's manufacturer horsepower agreement; export (US/EU) figures are generally considered closer to true output.

## Nissan Skyline GT-R (R34)
- Category: JDM Legends
- Years: 1999–2002
- Engine: RB26DETT, 2.6L twin-turbo inline-6
- Horsepower: 276 hp (officially — capped by Japan's "gentlemen's agreement"; real output widely believed higher)
- Torque: 293 lb-ft (392 Nm)
- 0–60 mph: 4.7 s
- Top speed: 156 mph (electronically limited)
- Curb weight: 3,439 lb (V-Spec and Nür trims run heavier)
- Drivetrain: ATTESA E-TS Pro AWD

## Mazda RX-7 (FD3S)
- Category: JDM Legends
- Years: 1992–2002
- Engine: 13B-REW, 1.3L twin-rotor sequential twin-turbo
- Horsepower: 255 hp (JDM base) — up to 280 hp on the late Spirit R
- Torque: 217 lb-ft
- 0–60 mph: 4.9 s
- Top speed: 155 mph (limited); Spirit R models to 161 mph
- Curb weight: ~2,800 lb
- Note: lightest car in this set, prized for 50/50 weight distribution.

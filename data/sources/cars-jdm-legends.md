# JDM Legends — Spec Comparison (Dataset 2)

> Verified via web search against published/enthusiast-site specs (August 2026). Where sources disagreed, the most commonly cited figure was used and noted. Treat 0–60 and top-speed figures as representative for the trim listed, not a single official factory number — these vary by market (JDM/US/EU), test methodology, and model year even for the same nameplate.

## Toyota Supra MK4 (A80) Turbo
- Years: 1993–2002
- Engine: 2JZ-GTE, 3.0L twin-turbo inline-6
- Horsepower: 321 hp
- Torque: 315 lb-ft
- 0–60 mph: 4.6 s
- Top speed: 155 mph (electronically limited; unlimited US models observed near 177 mph)
- Curb weight: 3,560 lb
- Note: JDM output was understated under Japan's manufacturer horsepower agreement; export (US/EU) figures are generally considered closer to true output.

## Nissan Skyline GT-R (R34)
- Years: 1999–2002
- Engine: RB26DETT, 2.6L twin-turbo inline-6
- Horsepower: 276 hp (officially — capped by Japan's "gentlemen's agreement"; real output widely believed higher)
- Torque: 293 lb-ft (392 Nm)
- 0–60 mph: 4.7 s
- Top speed: 156 mph (electronically limited)
- Curb weight: 3,439 lb (V-Spec and Nür trims run heavier)
- Drivetrain: ATTESA E-TS Pro AWD

## Mazda RX-7 (FD3S)
- Years: 1992–2002
- Engine: 13B-REW, 1.3L twin-rotor sequential twin-turbo
- Horsepower: 255 hp (JDM base) — up to 280 hp on the late Spirit R
- Torque: 217 lb-ft
- 0–60 mph: 4.9 s
- Top speed: 155 mph (limited); Spirit R models to 161 mph
- Curb weight: ~2,800 lb
- Note: lightest car in this set, prized for 50/50 weight distribution.

## Nissan Fairlady Z / 300ZX (Z32) Twin Turbo
- Years: 1989–2000 (JDM production; US sales ended 1996)
- Engine: VG30DETT, 3.0L twin-turbo V6
- Horsepower: 300 hp
- Torque: 283 lb-ft
- 0–60 mph: ~5.6–5.9 s (sources vary)
- Top speed: 155 mph (limited)
- Curb weight: 3,571 lb

## Nissan Fairlady Z / 370Z (Z34)
- Years: 2009–2020
- Engine: VQ37VHR, 3.7L naturally aspirated V6
- Horsepower: 324 hp standard / 350 hp (Nismo)
- Torque: 268 lb-ft standard / 276 lb-ft (Nismo)
- 0–60 mph: 5.1 s standard / 5.4 s (Nismo)
- Curb weight: 3,333 lb standard
- Drivetrain: naturally aspirated, no turbo — the outlier in this set alongside the NSX.

## Honda NSX (NA1 / NA2)
- Years: 1990–2005
- Engine: NA1 — C30A 3.0L V6 VTEC; NA2 — C32B 3.2L V6 VTEC (both naturally aspirated)
- Horsepower: 270 hp (NA1) / 276 hp (NA2)
- Torque: 209 lb-ft (NA1) / 220 lb-ft (NA2)
- 0–60 mph: 5.7 s (both generations)
- Curb weight: 3,020 lb (NA1; NA2 similar)
- Note: mid-engine, naturally aspirated — the only non-turbo/non-rotary car here besides the 370Z.

## Mitsubishi Lancer Evolution VI (CP9A)
- Years: 1999–2001
- Engine: 4G63T, 2.0L turbo inline-4
- Horsepower: 276 hp (280 PS)
- Torque: 275 lb-ft
- 0–60 mph: 4.4 s (GSR)
- Top speed: 150 mph
- Curb weight: 2,998 lb
- Drivetrain: AWD, rally-bred

## Subaru Impreza WRX STI (GC8) Version VI
- Years: 1999–2000
- Engine: EJ20, 2.0L turbo flat-4
- Horsepower: 280 PS (~276 hp)
- Torque: ~260 lb-ft (353 Nm)
- 0–60 mph: 6.4 s
- Curb weight: 2,800 lb (1,270 kg)
- Drivetrain: AWD, rally-bred
- Note: slowest 0–60 in this set despite similar power-to-weight peers — worth a look at why (gearing/AWD launch behavior of the era), a good real "surprising data point" for the chart demo.
